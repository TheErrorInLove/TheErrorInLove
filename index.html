The story

Sena Valentine accepts a scholarship at Korin Japanese International School (百星国際学) after her family faces financial struggles. She leaves her beloved school and friends behind, but her best friend, Jules Vance, comes along to support her.

In this unfamiliar environment, Sena joins the Council Club as a mandatory school activity. There, she must navigate the overwhelming personalities of six members, each with a different dere type.

One peaceful weekend, a mysterious email warns Sena that someone close to her is dangerously obsessed with her—and that she must distance herself immediately. Unsure who to trust, she starts pushing away the people she suspects may be dangerous.

Will Sena succumb to the pressure of loss, or protect the people she cares about before it is too late?

Behind the series

How did this all begin?

It actually started by accident! Our creator originally joined a WhatsApp group just to try out voice acting. At the time, the project was chaotic. Seeing the potential, she stepped in to help clean up the production and created a plot.

Over time, the original founders drifted away, leaving the project at a standstill. Instead of letting it die, she completely transformed our Discord server, took the reins, and kept pushing the story forward.

What started as a rough, unexpected takeover turned into a passion project when she realized how genuinely interested the community was. She kept going for the fans and we haven't stopped since (and hopefully will continue)!
